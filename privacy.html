Website and usage statistics

The Controller processes limited website statistics under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interests) to understand page use, public sources and campaigns, estimate daily audience size, and improve website content and service information. For this limited processing, the Controller relies on Art. 6(1)(f), rather than consent under Art. 6(1)(a).

Successful page requests increment aggregate counters by UTC day, canonical page, broad referral category and recognised shared campaign labels. Same-site referrals also provide aggregate page-to-page transition counts. Campaign labels identify a public campaign, not a recipient. Raw referral URLs, arbitrary query values, advertising click identifiers and individual event records are not retained.

To avoid treating every refresh as another visitor, the server derives a daily code from IP address and user-agent using HMAC-SHA-256 with a fresh random secret each UTC day. Raw IP addresses and user-agent values are not stored for analytics. The code is kept only in a separate day-and-code membership set to check whether it has already contributed to that day's site-wide visitor estimate. The secret is stored separately. Codes are not attached to pages, sources, campaigns or actions, returned to devices, or joined across days. The membership set cannot reconstruct browsing histories. Shared connections and browser changes affect these estimates; sums of daily estimates are not unique people over a longer period.

Daily codes are pseudonymised data within Art. 4(5). Pseudonymisation and separation are safeguards recognised by Arts. 25(1) and 32(1)(a); the processing basis is Art. 6(1)(f). Limited fields and short code retention implement data minimisation and storage limitation (Art. 5(1)(c), (e)).

Previous-day codes and secrets are deleted during request-time day rotation and scheduled cleanup. Aggregate counts are retained for 400 days. Live deletion does not immediately erase Cloudflare recovery copies, which expire under its backup retention. Reports are protected. Statistics are not linked to correspondence, sold, used for advertising, behavioural profiling or remarketing, or shared with advertising platforms.

No analytics cookies, browser storage, pixels, analytics scripts or extra browser requests are used. Email and external-link clicks are not measured. Known bots, prefetches and requests carrying Global Privacy Control (Sec-GPC: 1) or Do Not Track (DNT: 1) are excluded. Cloudflare processes technical request data to deliver and secure the website; security processing, including IP-based protection of reports, serves a separate purpose from statistics.

Email and retention

The Controller processes your email address, message, and attachments to answer enquiries: to take steps at your request before a contract where applicable (Art. 6(1)(b)), or for the Controller’s legitimate interest in handling general correspondence (Art. 6(1)(f)). Ordinary enquiries are kept as needed to answer and conclude the discussion. Providing information by email is voluntary; sufficient information is required to reply or arrange a service.

Relevant client correspondence is retained for contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b)) and, where necessary, establishing, exercising, or defending legal claims (Art. 6(1)(f)), until the applicable limitation period expires, accounting for extensions. Documents needed for tax and accounting obligations are retained for the applicable statutory periods (Art. 6(1)(c)). Tax records are generally kept for five years from the end of the calendar year in which tax payment was due, or longer where statutory periods are suspended or interrupted.

Your rights

You may object to processing based on legitimate interests under Art. 21.

Where personal data relating to the requester are held, for example in email correspondence, access, rectification, erasure, restriction or portability may be requested subject to the conditions of Arts. 15–20. Requests should be sent to am@jlogicsoftware.com. Where identity is reasonably in doubt, proportionate verification may be required before disclosure (Art. 12(6)).

The aggregate counters contain no visitor identifier and cannot identify a requester. Under Art. 11, the Controller need not collect additional data solely to identify a requester for these purposes. If the requester provides information enabling identification of personal data held by the Controller, applicable rights remain available. Complaints may be lodged with the Polish supervisory authority, UODO.
