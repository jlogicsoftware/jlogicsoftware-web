Engineering and leading teams

Technical work happens between people.

Building software and leading teams have taught me to look at technical decisions alongside business needs and the people doing the work. A solution needs to make sense in its context, and the reasoning behind it needs to be clear to others.

For example, in mentoring we can take one technical decision from your work and practise explaining the alternatives, its business impact, and your contribution.

From the interviewer’s side

I have conducted technical interviews as well as worked in engineering teams. That experience informs how I help developers prepare: explain what you did, discuss why you made a decision, and make your understanding clear through specific examples.

We can practise both the technical discussion and the way you present your experience, using situations from your own work.
